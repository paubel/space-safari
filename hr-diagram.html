How to read the diagram

Three patterns to look for

01

Follow the main sequence

Most stars form a diagonal band from hot, luminous blue stars to cool, faint red stars. This is where stars spend most of their lives fusing hydrogen.

02

Compare colour and heat

Blue-white stars on the left have hotter surfaces. Orange and red stars on the right are cooler—even when a giant’s large surface makes it extremely luminous.

03

Find the stellar outliers

Giants and supergiants sit above the main sequence. White dwarfs sit below it: very hot, but faint because their radiating surfaces are so small.

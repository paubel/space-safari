Star Map Filter

What does Star Map Filter do?

It replaces the decorative background stars with catalogued real stars that you can search, filter and inspect—without leaving the 3D planetarium.

  • Drag to look around and scroll to zoom.
  • Select a named star to see its physical properties.
  • Use location and time to show stars above your horizon.

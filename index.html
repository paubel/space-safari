🌌 Space Safari

A Journey Through Our Cosmic Neighborhood

Space Safari is a guided journey through our cosmic neighborhood — from the Sun and the familiar worlds of the Solar System to nearby stars, stellar nurseries, the Milky Way, distant galaxies, and the evolving universe.

The book has been extensively revised and expanded. Its science has been updated through July 2026, technical passages have been rewritten in clearer language, and important concepts now receive fuller explanations. The revised book also includes improved illustrations and diagrams, more consistent chapter summaries, an expanded glossary, and carefully reviewed sources.

Written for curious readers without requiring a background in astronomy, the book shows how planets, stars, interstellar clouds, galaxies, and cosmic history form one connected journey. This companion site adds images, videos, and selected resources. Read two complete chapters free, then continue the journey in the Kindle edition.

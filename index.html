🌌 Space Safari

A Journey Through Our Cosmic Neighborhood

Space Safari is your gateway to our cosmic neighborhood. This companion site expands every chapter with videos, images, and curated resources so the stories come alive beyond the page.

Preview the book on Amazon, then dive deeper into the Sun, the planets, nearby stars, and the Milky Way—follow the links to explore further and keep the sense of discovery going.

How to buy the Kindle edition

Find the book by searching for "Space Safari: A Journey Through Our Cosmic Neighborhood" on your local Amazon site:

  • US: amazon.com
  • UK: amazon.co.uk
  • DE (EU readers, use this): amazon.de
  • FR: amazon.fr
  • ES: amazon.es
  • IT: amazon.it
  • NL: amazon.nl
  • JP: amazon.co.jp
  • BR: amazon.com.br
  • CA: amazon.ca
  • MX: amazon.com.mx
  • AU: amazon.com.au
  • IN: amazon.in

If Kindle is not available in your region, purchase from the nearest store above—Amazon will deliver the Kindle edition to your account. For EU readers, use the DE store.

Read on any device

Once you own the Kindle edition, you can read it on:

  • Kindle devices: All e-readers (Kindle, Paperwhite, Oasis, etc.)
  • Tablets & phones: Download the free Kindle app for iOS, Android, iPad, or Android tablets
  • Computer: Read in your web browser at read.amazon.com
  • Mac & Windows: Install the Kindle app for desktop

Your books and notes sync instantly across all devices, so you can start reading on one device and continue on another.
